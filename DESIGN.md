# CodexPoker — The after-hours table

An intimate, tactile poker table in cream, felt green and terracotta. Ali and Codex are the two lead characters: matching portrait frames, expressive illustrated faces, stronger name/stack typography. Four local bots use compact geometric robot faces and quieter nameplates. The rivalry is friendly; poker information takes priority over flavor.

## Visual rules
- Warm paper #f3efe4, ink #293e36, felt #31584a, action #b95133.
- Manrope for identity/numerals, DM Sans for controls. Tabular chip counts.
- One table, one action surface. No in-app chat, model-provider labels, synthetic Elo or coaching panels.
- Codex and Ali remain visually prominent without suggesting a shared bankroll.
- All controls remain reachable at narrow widths. Fit the decision surface on laptop displays; adapt rather than shrink text.
- Cards use suit glyphs as well as color. Current turn is explicit in text and seat treatment.
- The server state paints immediately. Motion may explain a change but never hold actions hostage.
- Reactions depend only on public actions. No expression that reveals private hand strength.
- Respect reduced motion; keyboard focus stays visible. Review hand history on demand.

## Evaluation
A fresh observer should identify their seat, Codex, the bots, private hand, pot and action ownership without instructions. Blind evaluators are not told which seats should feel related. See docs/evals/PROTOCOL.md.
